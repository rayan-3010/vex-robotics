## The competition

VEX is the biggest school robotics competition in the world, with 20,000+ teams in 50+ countries. Every year there's a new game, and teams design, build, program and drive a robot to play it.

- Matches are 2v2: 15 seconds of autonomous (code only), then 1 min 45 s of driver control.
- Robots have to start inside an 18-inch cube and can use at most eight 11 W motors.

## What I did

**Lifting arm.** One season the scoring goal was twice the robot's height. Instead of using a full 11 W motor on the arm, I ran it on a single 5.5 W motor. I cut weight out of the aluminium C-channel and added plastic bracing for stiffness. The motor power saved went to the drivetrain, which I geared for speed so we'd reach contested game pieces first.

![Arm at full scoring height](https://rayan-pesnani.netlify.app/assets/img/vex-hero.webp)

**Stability.** Steel only at the most heavily loaded joints, aluminium everywhere else. The heaviest parts sat low in the chassis so the robot stayed planted when other robots pushed into it.
